# Credits

BlindRSS for iOS is the native SwiftUI port of [BlindRSS](https://github.com/serrebidev/BlindRSS), the screen-reader-first desktop RSS and podcast client. Same reader, rebuilt for VoiceOver.

Third-party work it builds on:

- [ios-swiftui-accessibility-techniques](https://github.com/cvs-health/ios-swiftui-accessibility-techniques) by CVS Health, for the accessibility patterns.
- AVPlayer for media playback.

Chromecast/DLNA casting and direct YouTube stream extraction are not ported: they need Google's Cast SDK and yt-dlp, neither of which runs on iOS.

Questions and bug reports are welcome in the [SerrebiProjects Telegram group](https://t.me/SerrebiProjects). More serrebi projects: [projects.serrebiradio.com](https://projects.serrebiradio.com).
